Clear Guidance

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything parents need to know about preparing for their studio visit.

Programs cater to children from 1 to 10 years old, organized by developmental stages:
• Toddler Time: Ages 1–3
• Little Explorers: Ages 3–5
• Junior Gymnastics: Ages 5–8
• Open Play: Ages 2–10

Sessions are booked directly through the PlayNest mobile app. In the app, you can select your child, review dietary or sensory preferences, choose your preferred session time, and instantly receive your digital contactless check-in pass.

For Toddler Time (ages 1–3), active parent or guardian participation is encouraged to support emotional confidence and motor exploration. For older groups (Little Explorers and Junior Gymnastics), parents may observe comfortably from our adjacent viewing lounge with dedicated tea and Wi-Fi.

Session management policies are configured in the mobile application trial. We respect the unpredictability of toddler life and early parenting routines, and session policies reflect clear guidelines inside your mobile booking profile.
